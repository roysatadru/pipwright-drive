Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Pipwright Drive is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account and computer.

  • With the owner's permission it uploads, lists, downloads and deletes the owner's own files in the owner's Google Drive folder Pipwright-Data. It does not read other Drive content.
  • No data is sent to anyone else, sold, shared, or used for advertising or model training. Google user data is used only to provide this storage function, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer only.
  • Access can be removed at any time in Google Account settings, under Security, Third-party connections.
